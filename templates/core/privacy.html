Privacy and Security

Patient information in EPRMS is confidential. Users may only access information needed for their authorized duties.

Role-based access
  • Receptionists: administrative patient information, registration, contact updates and appointments.
  • Nurses: relevant patient and nursing/clinical information.
  • Doctors: clinical information required for diagnosis and treatment.
  • Administrators: system administration and security/audit functions. Administrator status does not automatically grant clinical access.
Users must not
  • Share passwords.
  • Access records without a legitimate work purpose.
  • Photograph or copy patient records without authorization.
  • Leave an unlocked workstation displaying patient information.
  • Place diagnoses or sensitive clinical information in appointment reasons.
Security controls

EPRMS uses authentication, role-based authorization, CSRF protection, secure session settings, inactivity logout and audit logging. Production deployment should use HTTPS, encrypted storage/backups, strong secrets and appropriate organizational controls.

Data protection

The design follows privacy and security principles such as least privilege, data minimisation, purpose limitation and accountability. This student project should not be represented as a legal certification of POPIA compliance.
